Inventory Accounting

Stock In: Supplier deliveries increase inventory. SmartStock records the purchase price, total purchase value, and updated weighted-average cost.

Sales: Customer sales reduce inventory. Their purchase costs and gross profits are recorded separately in the Sales module.

Stock Out: Manual stock deductions record the inventory cost of the removed items. These should be classified separately if they represent transfers or corrections rather than actual losses.

Historical Records: Older movements without financial information remain unchanged. Missing values are displayed as a dash.
