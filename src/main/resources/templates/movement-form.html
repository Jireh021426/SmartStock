How this form works

Business Date: Enter the actual delivery or movement date, even when recording the transaction later. SmartStock also preserves when the entry was actually recorded.

Stock In: Adds purchased inventory directly to the selected location and updates the company total.

Purchase Cost: Enter the actual supplier cost. SmartStock calculates the updated company-wide weighted-average cost.

Stock Out: Records spoilage, damage or missing stock against the selected location.

Transfers & Sales: Do not use this form to move stock between company stalls. Use Transfers. Customer sales must be recorded using the appropriate sales workflow.
