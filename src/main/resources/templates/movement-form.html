Record Stock Movement

Stock In: Adds purchased inventory directly to the selected location and updates the company total. Supplier deliveries can go to any active stall or storage location.

Purchase cost: Enter the actual supplier cost. SmartStock calculates the updated company-wide weighted-average cost.

Stock Out: Records spoilage, damage or missing stock against the selected location.

Transfers & Sales: Do not use this form to move stock between stalls (use Transfers) or to record customer sales (use the Sales module).
