New Chicken Sale

Record a chicken sale from a specific stall or storage location. SmartStock deducts stock from that location and updates the company-wide inventory.

Only your assigned stall is available. Changing the request cannot bypass this restriction.

The displayed quantity is the company-wide stock total, not necessarily the quantity available at the selected stall.

Customer not registered? Register the customer first.

Selling Price: The current selling price is taken from the selected product.

Purchase Cost: SmartStock uses the product's weighted-average purchase cost to calculate gross profit.

Inventory: The sale is deducted from the selected location and the company total in one database transaction.
