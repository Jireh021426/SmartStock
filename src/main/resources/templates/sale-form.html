New Chicken Sale

Record a chicken sale from a specific stall or storage location.

No active cashier is assigned to a stall. Create and assign one under Employees before recording sales.

Selling Price: The current selling price is taken from the selected product.

Purchase Cost: SmartStock uses the product's weighted-average purchase cost to calculate gross profit.

Inventory: The sale is deducted from the selected location and the company total in one database transaction.
