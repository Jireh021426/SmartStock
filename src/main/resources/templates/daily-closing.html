Do not count shortages as sales automatically. Differences between physical and book stock may come from missing transactions, spoilage, weighing mistakes or theft. Potential revenue from unexplained quantities is not recognized revenue or cash collected. Payment collections and cashier remittances must be checked in their own modules. Independent-stall deliveries are excluded; they are recorded separately as credit sales.
