SmartStock Independent Stalls Update (local-first)

IMPORTANT: Stop the local SmartStock server before extracting.
Apply the existing business-date migration first if not already applied:
  database/migrations/2026-11-20_stock_movement_business_date.sql
Then apply:
  database/migrations/2026-11-20_independent_stalls.sql
Run focused tests with `mvn -Dtest=IndependentStallFlowTest test`.
Then run the full suite `mvn test` before committing or deploying.

New pages (ADMIN or MANAGER only):
 /independent-stalls
 /independent-stalls/new
 /independent-stalls/deliveries/new
 /independent-stalls/payments/new

Each independent-stall delivery is one CREDIT sale and one SALE-category
stock movement. The delivery deducts company source inventory and charges
the customer's full value; no destination company stock is created.
Payments reduce the debt and have no cashier attribution, therefore are
excluded from cashier remittances.

Limitations: backdated deliveries adjust present inventory immediately;
historical closing stock and historical weighted-average cost are NOT
reconstructed. Daily expenses still use entered timestamps. Do not deploy
to Railway until both database migrations and the entire test suite pass.
